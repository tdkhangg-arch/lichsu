## Description
October 1, 331 BC. On a flat, dusty plain in what is now Iraq, a 25-year-old king from Macedonia faced the largest army of the ancient world. Darius III had chosen the ground, leveled it for his bladed chariots and brought soldiers from three continents. Alexander had about 47,000 men. By sunset, the Persian Empire was finished.

A father tells his son the story of the Battle of Gaugamela: the night Alexander refused to "steal" his victory, the chariot charge that went nowhere, and the gap that opened right in front of the Persian king.
